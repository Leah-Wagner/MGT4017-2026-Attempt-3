    Gardening Center

    Explore how to grow food, care for your garden, or troubleshoot plant problems.

    Where can you get gardening supplies?

    Try local garden centres, hardware shops, community gardens, seed swaps, and online suppliers. Start with a pot, compost, and a few easy seeds or herbs.

    Common gardening problems people have

    Yellow leaves can be caused by watering issues, poor drainage, or lack of nutrients. Check the soil and light first, then change one thing at a time.

    Here's how you can start your gardening journey

    Start small: choose a sunny spot, beginner-friendly herbs, containers with drainage, and water when the top of the soil feels dry.
